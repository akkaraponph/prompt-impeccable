# Form UX

/impeccable

Improve this form for clarity, speed, and usability.

Review:

* field ordering
* labels
* helper text
* placeholders
* field width
* grouping
* spacing
* required states
* validation
* error messages
* disabled states
* submit actions

Reduce unnecessary explanation.

Do not use placeholders as replacements for labels.

Group related fields logically.

Make errors actionable and specific.

Avoid placing every field inside its own card.
