# Dark Mode Audit

/impeccable

Refine dark mode.

Avoid simply converting white surfaces into black surfaces.

Review:

* surface hierarchy
* border contrast
* muted text
* selected states
* hover states
* shadows
* status colors
* readability

Avoid excessive pure black.

Avoid glowing borders and neon effects unless they are part of the established design language.

Dark mode should feel calm and comfortable during long usage.
