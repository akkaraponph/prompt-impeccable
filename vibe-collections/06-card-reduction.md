# Card Reduction

/impeccable

Audit all cards and containers on this interface.

For every card, determine whether a visual container is actually necessary.

Flatten unnecessary cards.

Avoid:

* card inside card
* every section becoming a card
* excessive borders
* excessive shadows
* oversized radius
* decorative surfaces

Prefer:

* sections
* lists
* rows
* whitespace
* subtle separators
* typography

Keep cards only where they provide meaningful grouping.
